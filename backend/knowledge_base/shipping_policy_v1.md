# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Policy version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-31

## Dispatch and delivery estimates

In-stock orders are generally dispatched within 1–2 business days after
confirmation. Delivery is usually estimated at 2–5 business days for major
metro service areas and 5–8 business days for other serviceable Indian
pin codes after dispatch. Remote locations, public holidays, weather,
carrier capacity, and serviceability can change these estimates.

These are sample estimates, not a live promise for an individual order. Use
the order record or a verified carrier update for order-specific information.
The assistant must not invent a tracking number or delivery event.
Any shipping charge in ₹ is shown at checkout before the order is placed;
the charge depends on the destination and delivery option.

## Delayed or undelivered orders

If an order appears delayed, sign in and check its recorded status. Contact
support with the order ID if the status does not resolve the issue. Support
can investigate with the carrier and share a verified update.

it is not legal advice or a statement of a real merchant's terms.
