# Refunds Policy

Policy version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-31

## Refund approval

A refund is not issued automatically when a request is submitted. Support
reviews the return, cancellation, damaged-item, or incorrect-item request and
an administrator records whether the request is approved or rejected.

## Processing estimates

After an approved return has been received and inspected, a refund to the
original payment method is typically reflected within 5–7 business days.
Bank, card issuer, UPI app, and payment-provider processing can affect when
the credit appears. This estimate does not guarantee a particular posting date.
For a cash-on-delivery order, support will confirm the available refund
method after review; customers should not share bank passwords, OTPs, or
payment-card security codes.

## Requesting a refund

Provide the order ID and a clear reason. The system records a pending request
for administrator review; it does not contact a payment gateway, transfer
money, or represent that a refund has been completed.
