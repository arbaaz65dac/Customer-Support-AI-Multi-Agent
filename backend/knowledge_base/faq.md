
# Customer Support FAQ

## Contacting Customer Support

Customers can contact the support team by submitting a support ticket through the customer support application. They should include a clear subject and a detailed description of their issue.

## Checking an Order

Customers can ask for the current status of an existing order. The application should retrieve the status from its order database rather than guessing.

## Product Availability

Product availability is based on the stock quantity recorded in the product catalogue. A product with zero stock should not be described as currently available.

## Product Prices

Product prices should be taken from the current product catalogue. The assistant must not invent prices or promise discounts that are not recorded in the system.
