# Warranty Policy

Policy version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-31

## Coverage

For this sample store, eligible electronics carry a limited 12-month
manufacturer warranty from the invoice date unless the product listing or
manufacturer warranty card states a different period. The manufacturer or
authorised service centre determines coverage after inspection.

## Exclusions and evidence

Accidental or liquid damage, normal wear, unauthorised repair, consumables,
and damage caused by misuse may not be covered. Keep the invoice, serial
number, accessories, and original packaging where available. Warranty
coverage is not a promise of replacement or refund before inspection.

## Requesting assistance

Provide the order ID, product name, a description of the fault, and when it
started. Support can record a ticket and direct the customer to the relevant
service process. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or payment security codes.

This versioned document is sample project policy content for demonstration;
it is not legal advice or a statement of a real merchant's terms.
