# Customer Support FAQ

Policy version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-31

## How do I contact support?

Sign in and create a support ticket with a clear subject and description.
Include the relevant order ID when the question concerns an order. An
administrator reviews tickets and can reply or update their status.

## How do I check an order?

Sign in and view My Orders, or ask the assistant to check an order. The
application verifies that the order belongs to the signed-in customer before
returning its recorded status.

## Where do product price and stock details come from?

Current product prices and stock counts come from the product catalogue.
Stock may change; a displayed count is not a reservation or guarantee.

## How do I request a return or refund?

Provide the order ID and reason for a request. Return and refund requests are
recorded for administrator review and are not approved or paid automatically.
